🌐 RISK TWIN OSS: Causally-Constrained World Model Simulation & Macro Stress-Testing 🛡️

⚙️ SYSTEM CORE & FOUNDATION

✨ RISK TWIN OSS builds a causally-constrained simulation environment (a 🧠 World Model) to train and evaluate Reinforcement Learning (RL) agents for supply chain and retail optimization. By combining formal Causal Inference with RL this platform simulates extreme macroeconomic shocks (🌪️ Era Swaps) to stress-test logistics and inventory policies under volatile frontier regimes.

🛒 RETAIL TWIN DOMAIN

🏪 Domain 1: Walmart (Retail Operations)

  • 🎯 Causal Mechanism: Tracks how external labor markets (👥 Unemployment) and inflationary pressures (🏷️ CPI) drive retail inventory stockout risks.
  • 🌱 Baseline State (2026 DNA): Stable operational conditions with an expected baseline stockout rate of 4.2%.
  • 🔥 Stress Regimes: Evaluates severe labor-isolation shocks (📉 2008 GFC) and compounded dual-shocks (🦠 COVID-19 Retail Era).
🚢 LOGISTICS TWIN DOMAIN

📦 Domain 2: DataCo (Logistics Operations)

  • 🎯 Causal Mechanism: Tracks how port congestion (⚓ NY Fed GSCPI) paired with global energy costs (⛽ Fuel Price) impact delivery latency.
  • 🌱 Baseline State (2026 Routes): High inherent routing friction with a baseline late delivery risk of 54.3%.
  • 🔥 Stress Regimes: Simulates multi-port gridlocks trade embargoes and acute global supply bottlenecks.

📚 Notebook & Component Deep Dive

📓 Core Research Notebooks

  • 🛠️ Setup.ipynb: Configures runtime environment installs critical dependencies (xlrd, openpyxl) and streams live macroeconomic datasets including the NY Fed GSCPI index.
  • 📊 DataCo Supply Chain EDA.ipynb: Performs exploratory data analysis on shipping routes establishes late delivery distributions and isolates missing data anomalies.
  • 🕸️ causal_graph.ipynb: Formulates Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs) and Structural Causal Models (SCMs) linking macro variables to transit lateness to neutralize confounding bias.
  • 🧠 world_model.ipynb: Trains the environment transition dynamics model to generate high-fidelity synthetic counterfactual trajectories for policy training.

⚙️ Simulation Engines & Baselines

  • 🌪️ era_swap.ipynb: Implements the counterfactual engine that injects macroeconomic shocks (e.g. COVID-2020 logistics stress or 2008 financial shocks) into current operational states.
  • 🎮 simulators.ipynb: Wraps world models and era-swapping mechanics into standard step-action-reward interfaces compatible with RL frameworks.
  • 🚀 risk_twin_pipeline.ipynb: Unifies data ingestion causal graph construction world modeling and simulation into an automated end-to-end execution pipeline.
  • 📏 sc_ss_policy.ipynb: Implements classical (s, S) inventory policies as an empirical benchmark to quantify the performance gains of Causal RL algorithms.
